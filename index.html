The Scenario ← change

You need a £1,200 laptop.

Yours just died. You need one for work or school. You have no savings toward it yet, a small £150 cushion already sitting in your current account, and about £70/month of spare cash you could put toward this if you choose to save. Pick how you'll pay for it — then live out 24 months and see what each choice actually costs, in pounds and in credit score.

All three paths start from the same place: a credit score of 700 (Good), and — if you borrow — a card limit of £3,000. If a payment ever costs more than your cushion plus that month's spare cash, you'll have to decide how to cover it — pay what you can, overdraft your account, or charge it to a backup card. Each costs you differently.

⚠ This run includes a job-loss shock: for 2 months partway through, your spare cash drops to £0 — no matter which path you're on.
